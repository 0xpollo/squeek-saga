How it works

  1. The wheel settles

    Squeektoshi's wheel work is grouped into 600-second rounds and settled on SqueekCore, on Ethereum Sepolia.

  2. The hour is read

    Once six rounds in a row are certified, their settled work decides the chapter's mood: slept, trotted or sprinted.

  3. The company votes

    Between chapters, members vote with Discord buttons on what the hero does. A tie is broken by the wheel.

  4. The chapter is written

    The storyteller writes a short chapter that follows the vote, names the players who took part, and links to its settlement transaction.

Rounds that SqueekCore has not settled yet are unknown, not zero. The saga waits for them.

Play it in Discord

Everything happens in your server with slash commands and buttons. No wallet, no sign-up, nothing to pay.

/join
Create your character: Seedblade, Lanternwright, Mosswhisper, Tunnelrunner, Crumbmage or Storykeeper.
Vote buttons
Under every chapter. One vote each, changeable until the next chapter. Ties are broken by the wheel's settled work.
/prep
Once a day, get ready for the weekly boss. You earn glory for every work unit the hamster settles in the next 24 hours.
/raid
The boss, the damage dealt by settled work, and how many rounds are still unknown.
/me
Your character sheet: votes, glory, watch streak, and what the storyteller remembers about you.
/leaderboard
Glory rankings inside your server, and servers ranked against each other.
/wheel
What SqueekCore has certified so far and when the next chapter can land.
/setup
For admins: pick the channel where each new chapter is posted.
/forget
Erase your character and everything stored about you. See privacy.
